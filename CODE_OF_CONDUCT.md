# Code of conduct

Be kind, be direct, assume good faith. Argue about designs, not people. Security review is blunt by nature — "this leaks a token" is a fact, not an insult — so expect and give frank technical feedback, and keep it about the code.

Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space (issues, PRs, discussions, chat). Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock content and may ban repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the address on the org profile. Reports are handled privately.

This is a short-form adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1; that document governs anything not covered here.
